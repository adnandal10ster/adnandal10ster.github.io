Our Mission & Vision

Grounded in Islamic values, we serve communities with dignity, transparency, and ihsan.

Mission

We work to share a true understanding of Islam across England, Wales, and Bangladesh — nurturing spiritual growth, promoting compassion, and serving humanity. Guided by faith and a commitment to public benefit, we support underprivileged students, assist people facing hardship, and help build educational and religious institutions that strengthen communities with dignity, hope, and care.

Vision

A world where faith and service stand together — where mosques, schools, and community programmes light the way with knowledge, unity, and hope. We aspire to see communities empowered through education and guided by Islamic values, creating an environment where every individual can flourish with dignity, purpose, and opportunity.

Who We Are

Serving communities with dignity, transparency, accountability, and ihsan across the UK and Bangladesh.

QNS Al-Khayr is rooted in local relationships and trusted community partners. We channel your generosity into mosques, schools, family welfare, food aid, and life-changing medical support. Every project is designed with accountability, clear reporting, and an emphasis on sustainable benefit.

We believe that compassion guided by Islamic principles can uplift families and entire communities. From student sponsorships to clean water wells, we prioritise dignity, transparency, and long-term impact.

Impact & Stories

Donations become real projects—new masjids, wells, scholarships, and medical support.

Every contribution is tracked and reported. Communities receive the support they ask for, and we work with local partners to ensure funds are used effectively and with ihsan. From emergency aid to long-term education, your giving is turning hardship into hope.

Rebuilt Masjid

A coastal masjid reopened with reinforced walls, new wudu areas, and evening Qur’an circles for youth.

Scholarship Success
